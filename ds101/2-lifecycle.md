## 🧠 Key Takeaways

- The lifecycle is not just a technical flow — it’s a **collaborative, iterative process**.
- Framing the problem correctly is more important than rushing to a solution.
- **Stakeholder alignment** is essential — the problem must matter to the organization.
- **Curiosity and creativity** drive better framing and more meaningful analysis.
- The lifecycle is not linear — expect loops, pivots, and reframing.
- **Communication is part of the lifecycle** — insights must be delivered with clarity and impact.
- The lifecycle is a **team sport** — no single person owns every phase.
- **Tooling should support lifecycle clarity**, not obscure it.

---

## ✨ Reflection

This module reframed the lifecycle as a human-centered process. It’s not just about data wrangling and modeling — it’s about asking the right questions, aligning with stakeholders, and communicating findings in ways that drive action. The lifecycle is iterative, messy, and collaborative. That’s not a flaw — it’s a feature.

---

## 📅 2017 vs. 2025 Perspective

| **2017 Framing** | **2025 Reality** |
|------------------|------------------|
| Lifecycle is a linear sequence | Lifecycle is iterative and nonlinear |
| Problem framing is technical | Problem framing is strategic and stakeholder-driven |
| Communication is a final step | Communication is embedded throughout |
| Tools drive the lifecycle | People and purpose drive the lifecycle |
